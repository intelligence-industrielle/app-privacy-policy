**Support — Andon**

Need help with the Andon app? Write to **achats@intelligenceindustrielle.com** with your organization, your phone (model, iOS or Android) and the app version (App settings → About). We usually answer within one business day.

**Frequently asked questions**

**I can't sign in.**
Your account is created by your organization. Sign in with the same account as on the web (for example your work Microsoft account). If access is denied, ask your plant's Andon administrator to check your account.

**I don't get call notifications.**
Check that Andon notifications are allowed in your phone's settings, and that Do Not Disturb or Focus isn't blocking them. In the app: App settings → Notifications.

**How do I open a workstation console (Station mode)?**
Scan the QR code shown at the workstation with the app (scan button). The workstation console opens.

**The app asks for Face ID or my fingerprint.**
That's the app lock, which you turned on. Turn it off in App settings → Security.

**The app shows "No network".**
The app then shows the latest known calls and retries on its own as soon as the network is back. You can also tap "Retry".

**I'm changing phones.**
Install Andon on the new phone and sign in. To remove the old one, ask your administrator to disconnect it.
