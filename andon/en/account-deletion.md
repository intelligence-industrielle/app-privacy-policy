# Account deletion — Andon

This page explains how to delete your Andon account and what is deleted. Andon is an application by Intelligence Industrielle inc. for the employees of its client organizations.

## 1. From the application (recommended)

1.  Open **Andon** and sign in.
2.  Tap the **circle with your initials**, at the top right (your profile menu).
3.  Tap **Delete my account**.
4.  To confirm, type **DELETE**, then tap **Delete permanently**.

Deletion is **immediate and permanent**: you are signed out of all your devices and can no longer sign in with this account. It cannot be undone.

## 2. If you no longer have access to the application

Write to **achats@intelligenceindustrielle.com**, subject "Andon account deletion", from your account's email address. We delete the account and confirm it within 30 days. Your organization, which manages your account, can also delete it.

## 3. What is deleted

*   **Your account**: name, email address, role and access, on the Intelligence Industrielle platform.
*   **Your sessions**: all your sign-ins, on all your devices, are closed.
*   **Your phone**: it is removed from Andon notifications.
*   **On the device**: your sign-in tokens are erased right away; other data kept by the application (settings, latest offline calls) is removed when you uninstall it.

## 4. What is kept

*   **Your organization's work data**: Andon calls (workstation, call type, times, comments) belong to your organization and stay in its history, according to its contract with Intelligence Industrielle.
*   **Your entry in the plant's team** (name, phone for alerts), created by your organization: it is removed by an administrator of your organization, or by us on request at the address above.
