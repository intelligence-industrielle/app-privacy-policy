**Privacy Policy — Andon**

This policy applies to the Andon mobile application (the "Application"), published by Intelligence Industrielle inc. ("we"). The Application gives phone access to a client plant's Andon system: help calls from workstations, follow-up and notifications. It is provided free of charge to employees of Intelligence Industrielle's client organizations; each person's account is created by their organization, never in the Application, and everyone can delete their own account from the Application.

**What the Application collects**

*   **Account**: name, email address and role, received from your organization or its identity provider (for example Microsoft) when you sign in.
*   **Work data**: the Andon calls you create, take or close (workstation, call type, times, comments). They belong to your organization.
*   **Device**: an Application-specific device identifier, the notification token, the Application version, the system (iOS or Android) and the language. They link your phone to your account and deliver call notifications.
*   **Usage**: pages viewed, actions, session length and errors, measured by Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to improve the service. Microsoft Clarity links these measurements to your name or email, for support.
*   **Voice (only when you dictate)**: when you tap the microphone to dictate a cause or an instruction, the recording is sent, through our servers, to Google Gemini to be transcribed into text (see "Artificial intelligence features"). It is used only to produce that text; the microphone is never used without your action.

The Application does **not** collect your location, contacts or photos.

**Artificial intelligence features (Google Gemini)**

Four Andon features use Google's artificial intelligence (Gemini model), through the Intelligence Industrielle platform. **Before the first transfer, the Application shows you what will be sent and to whom, and asks for your consent** ("Accept" or "Refuse"). Without your consent, nothing is sent and these features stay off; the rest of Andon works normally. Your choice is kept on the device; after a refusal, the Application asks again when you open one of these features.

| Feature | Data sent to Google Gemini | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AI analysis (Analytics) | the text of comments and causes entered when calls are resolved | to summarize them and find themes |
| Configuration assistant | what you write to the assistant, and the plant configuration it reads (departments, workstations, call types, teams, responders' names and contact details) | to answer and propose changes, which you confirm |
| Voice dictation | the recording of your voice | to turn it into text |
| Suggested icon | the name of a call type | to suggest an icon |

This data is used only to produce the requested answer. We use the paid Gemini API: under [Google's terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms), Google processes this data on our behalf, as a processor, under its Data Processing Addendum, does not use it to improve its products or train its models, and keeps requests only for a limited period, to detect abuse and meet legal obligations. This protection is at least equivalent to the one described in this policy. We never send Google your password or your phone's data.

**What stays on your phone**

*   **Camera**: used only to read the QR code of a workstation or an Andon link. No image is saved or sent.
*   **Face ID / fingerprint**: if you turn on the lock, your phone performs the check; we receive no biometric data.
*   **Offline**: the latest open calls and your settings are kept on the device, to show without a network.

**Why we use this data**

To sign you in, show and handle your plant's calls, send you call notifications, keep the service secure, answer support requests and improve the Application. We do not sell your data, show ads or track you across apps.

**Who has access**

Your organization (according to the rights it grants in Andon) and Intelligence Industrielle, to provide the service. Our processors, each for its own task only: Vercel (web application hosting), Expo, Apple and Google / Firebase (notification delivery), Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity (usage measurement), Google Gemini (artificial intelligence features, only with your consent). We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

Third-party policies: [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy), [Expo](https://expo.dev/privacy), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Microsoft](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).

**Retention**

Work and account data are kept while your organization uses Andon, then deleted or anonymized according to its contract. A phone's registration is deleted when it is disconnected. Data kept on the device is removed when you uninstall the Application.

**Your rights**

Under Québec's Law 25, PIPEDA (Canada) and the GDPR (Europe), you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, and withdraw your consent. Write to us; we answer within 30 days. Your organization, which manages your account, can also handle the request.

**Delete your account**: in the Application, your profile menu → "Delete my account". Deletion is immediate and permanent. Steps, deleted and kept data: [account deletion](https://github.com/intelligence-industrielle/app-privacy-policy/blob/main/andon/en/account-deletion.md).

**Security**

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), role-based access, phones linked by a single-use personal code and a device-specific certificate.

**Children**

The Application is intended for employees, not for people under 16.
