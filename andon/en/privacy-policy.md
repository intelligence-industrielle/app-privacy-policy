**Privacy Policy — Andon**

This policy applies to the Andon mobile application (the "Application"), published by Intelligence Industrielle inc. ("we"). The Application gives phone access to a client plant's Andon system: help calls from workstations, follow-up and notifications. It is provided free of charge to employees of Intelligence Industrielle's client organizations; each person's account is created by their organization, never in the Application.

**What the Application collects**

*   **Account**: name, email address and role, received from your organization or its identity provider (for example Microsoft) when you sign in.
*   **Work data**: the Andon calls you create, take or close (workstation, call type, times, comments). They belong to your organization.
*   **Device**: an Application-specific device identifier, the notification token, the Application version, the system (iOS or Android) and the language. They link your phone to your account and deliver call notifications.
*   **Usage**: pages viewed, actions, session length and errors, measured by Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to improve the service. Microsoft Clarity links these measurements to your name or email, for support.
*   **Voice (only when you dictate)**: when you tap the microphone to dictate a cause or an instruction, the recording is sent to our servers to be transcribed into text. It is used only to produce that text; the microphone is never used without your action.

The Application does **not** collect your location, contacts or photos.

**What stays on your phone**

*   **Camera**: used only to read the QR code of a workstation or an Andon link. No image is saved or sent.
*   **Face ID / fingerprint**: if you turn on the lock, your phone performs the check; we receive no biometric data.
*   **Offline**: the latest open calls and your settings are kept on the device, to show without a network.

**Why we use this data**

To sign you in, show and handle your plant's calls, send you call notifications, keep the service secure, answer support requests and improve the Application. We do not sell your data, show ads or track you across apps.

**Who has access**

Your organization (according to the rights it grants in Andon) and Intelligence Industrielle, to provide the service. Our processors, each for its own task only: Vercel (web application hosting), Expo, Apple and Google / Firebase (notification delivery), Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity (usage measurement). We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

Third-party policies: [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy), [Expo](https://expo.dev/privacy), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Microsoft](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).

**Retention**

Work and account data are kept while your organization uses Andon, then deleted or anonymized according to its contract. A phone's registration is deleted when it is disconnected. Data kept on the device is removed when you uninstall the Application.

**Your rights**

Under Québec's Law 25, PIPEDA (Canada) and the GDPR (Europe), you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, and withdraw your consent. Write to us; we answer within 30 days. Your organization, which manages your account, can also handle the request.

**Security**

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), role-based access, phones linked by a single-use personal code and a device-specific certificate.

**Children**

The Application is intended for employees, not for people under 16.

Intelligence Industrielle inc., person in charge of the protection of personal information: achats@intelligenceindustrielle.com
