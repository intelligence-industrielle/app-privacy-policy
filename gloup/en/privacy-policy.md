**Privacy Policy — Gloup**

*Effective November 13, 2026.*

Gloup (the "Application") is a French-language educational games app for children aged 2 to 7, published by Intelligence Industrielle inc. ("we"). It is designed so that no information about your child ever leaves your device.

**What stays on your device**

*   **Child profiles**: the first name or nickname you choose, the month and year of birth (to adapt activities to the child's age), and an avatar.
*   **Play**: progress in activities, stars, rewards, diplomas, the child's wishes ("Ask a grown-up") and your settings.

This information is stored **only on your device**. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can edit or delete a profile in the parents' area; deleting the Application erases everything.

**What the Application does not do**

*   No account to create, no sign-in.
*   No advertising, no tracking, no analytics or audience measurement.
*   No artificial intelligence during play: Gloup's voice is recorded in advance and included in the Application, which works offline.
*   No access to the microphone, camera, photos, contacts or location.

**What you choose to share**

The parents' area, protected by a question for adults, lets you **share a diploma as an image** or **copy a progress backup** (to restore it on another device). These go through your device's share sheet, to the destination you choose; we do not receive them.

**Subscription (Gloup Plus, Gloup Famille)**

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store (or Google Play). We never see your payment information. To check the subscription, the Application uses **RevenueCat**, which receives an anonymous identifier specific to the Application, the history of purchases made in the Application and technical information (device model and operating system, Application version, store country). RevenueCat receives no information about the child. This information is used only to unlock what you paid for and to restore it on your devices.

**Updates**

The Application checks with **Expo** (our update provider) whether a new version of its content is available. This check contains no information about you or your child.

**Who has access**

No one at our company has access to profiles or progress, which stay on your device. Our service providers, each for its sole task: Apple and Google (purchases), RevenueCat (subscription checks), Expo (updates). They may process technical information outside Quebec (notably in the United States); we choose them and bind them so that this information receives adequate protection there. We may disclose information if required by law.

**Retention**

Profiles and progress stay on the device until you delete them. Purchase history is kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own rules and tax obligations.

**Children**

Gloup is meant for children but collects no personal information about them: nothing is sent off the device. Purchases, links and sharing are reserved for adults, behind the parents' area question. We comply with Quebec's Law 25, PIPEDA (Canada), COPPA (United States) and the GDPR (Europe).

**Your rights**

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your information, and withdraw your consent. Since we keep no information about you or your child, deleting profiles in the Application, or the Application itself, is enough. For purchase history, write to us; we reply within 30 days.

**Controller and contact**

Intelligence Industrielle inc., person in charge of the protection of personal information: achats@intelligenceindustrielle.com

**Changes**

We will post any change on this page, with its effective date.
