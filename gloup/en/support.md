**Support — Gloup**

Need help with Gloup? Write to **achats@intelligenceindustrielle.com** with your device (iPhone, iPad or Android) and the Application version (Parents' area → Privacy and help). We usually reply within one business day.

**Frequently asked questions**

**How do I open the parents' area?**
Tap the padlock on the home screen, then answer the question for adults (a multiplication).

**What is free?**
39 complete activities, with no ads and no time limit, one story and one activity from the seasonal world. The other activities offer a discovery round; after that, the child can "Ask a grown-up".

**What do Gloup Plus and Gloup Famille include?**
Every activity and the seasonal worlds, every story, the Gloup Plus wardrobe collection and full progress reports. Gloup Plus: 3 child profiles. Gloup Famille: 6 profiles and Family Sharing.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Gloup → Cancel Subscription. To pay nothing after the free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. The subscription stays active until the end of the paid period.

**I changed devices and my subscription is gone.**
Parents' area → the subscription line at the top ("Gloup gratuit") → "Restore purchases", with the same App Store account used for the purchase.

**How do I get progress back on a new device?**
Progress stays on the device. On the old one: Parents' area → Transfer progress → Copy the backup, then send it (Messages, AirDrop, Notes). On the new one: same screen → Paste the backup.

**Does Gloup work offline?**
Yes. Gloup's voice is included in the Application.
